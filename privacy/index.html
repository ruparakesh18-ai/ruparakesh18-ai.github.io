Privacy Policy

Explained Simply and Explained Simply Uploader · Last updated 6 November 2026

What the app is

Explained Simply Uploader is a private tool that uploads videos made by Explained Simply to the Explained Simply YouTube channel and Instagram account. Only the channel owner uses it.

What data it uses

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth, scope youtube.upload), the app uploads videos and sets their title, description, tags and release time on the owner's own YouTube channel. It does not read, collect or store data about viewers or any other Google users.

Where data is kept

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is never sold, shared or sent anywhere except to Google (YouTube) and Meta (Instagram) to upload our own videos.

Google API Services

Explained Simply Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
